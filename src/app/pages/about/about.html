About us

“Jbalna” means “our mountains”.

Tunisia has wild ridges, pine and oak forests and summits close to 1,300 m, yet hiking here still runs on Facebook groups and word of mouth. Jbalna gives the mountains one meeting point.

Why we built it

  • Scattered information

    Hikes are shared in chats and groups, with no reliable sheet: distance, elevation, difficulty.

  • Uncertain safety

    Going alone on Djebel Ressas or Djebel Serj means risks with the route, the terrain and the weather.

  • Invisible guides

    Local guides live on word of mouth and have no shop window to publish their routes.

It started with our own hikes

We are hikers first. The photos on this site were taken by us and our friends on Zaghouan, Ressas, Serj and Bargou, the four mountains Jbalna starts with.
