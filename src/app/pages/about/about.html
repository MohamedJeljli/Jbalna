About us

“Jbalna” means “our mountains”.

Tunisia has wild ridges, cedar and oak forests and a summit at 1,544 m, yet hiking here still runs on Facebook groups and word of mouth. Jbalna gives the mountains one meeting point.

Why we built it

  • Scattered information

    Hikes are shared in chats and groups, with no reliable sheet: distance, elevation, difficulty.

  • Uncertain safety

    Going alone on Djebel Ressas or Djebel Serj means risks with the route, the terrain and the weather.

  • Invisible guides

    Local guides live on word of mouth and have no shop window to publish their routes.
